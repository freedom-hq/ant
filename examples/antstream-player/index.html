Channel

Paste the channel an AntStream broadcaster shared (the whole text the app's Share button sends will do): their address and the channel's topic, 0x…/<topic> (the topic as text, or as 64 hex digits for a channel without a text topic), their address alone for their app's channel, or a Solar Punk swarm://<owner>/<topic> link. The player follows the channel's Swarm feed and keeps you at the live edge; a finished broadcast plays back from the start.

    Served from Swarm · plays through your browser's own node in Freedom, or through any bee/ant gateway.
