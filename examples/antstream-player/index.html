    Help

    • A channel is a broadcaster's address (0x and 40 hex digits) and a topic. The field takes:
      • 0x<owner>/<topic text>
      • 0x<owner>/<64-hex topic>, for a channel without a text topic
      • 0x<owner> alone, for the app's antstream channel
      • a Solar Punk link, swarm://<owner>/<topic>
      • the whole text an AntStream app's Share button sends
    • A live channel starts at the live edge. A finished broadcast plays from the start.
    • Channels you watched drop down under the field. Pick one with a click, or the arrow keys and Enter; × or Delete removes it.
    • The player plays through your browser's own Swarm node in Freedom, or through any bee/ant gateway.
